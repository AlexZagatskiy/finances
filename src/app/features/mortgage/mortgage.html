Keep the assumptions in view

Property price minus own contribution determines the loan. Both installment plans use the same amount, duration and interest scenario. Equal installments are recalculated on the remaining balance and remaining term when the assumed rate changes. Decreasing installments keep the same principal portion.

Interest uses the outstanding balance × annual nominal rate ÷ 12, rounded to a grosz monthly. The final payment adjusts for rounding. Actual bank day counts and settlement amounts may differ. This is a budget estimate, not a bank quote, creditworthiness assessment or RRSO calculation.

Insurance may change or apply for only part of the term; this calculator uses the constant monthly cost you enter. Construction tranches, grace periods, subsidies and overpayments are not modeled. LTV is informational and does not establish eligibility or a required minimum contribution.

For your offer, check the interest and margin, fixed period, fees, insurance and account conditions. Bank product examples reviewed on 2 November 2026: PKO Bank Polski (opens in a new tab) and ING (opens in a new tab). Rates are entered by you; no offers are downloaded.
